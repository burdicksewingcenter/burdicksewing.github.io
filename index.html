Proud distributor of Viking, Riccar, Elna, Simplicity, and Singer.

Don Burdick started on Third Ave. at The Singer Store in 1957. He started Burdick's Sewing and Vacuum Center in 1983 where we sell the best and service the rest. We are a family-owned business. Today the business includes wife Joan and son Darren.

We sell Husqvarna sewing, embroidery, and overlock machines up to 5 threads. We also carry White and Elna embroidery and overlock machines.

In our vacuum cleaner line we carry Royal, Panasonic, Miele, and Eureka. We also stock a full line of sewing notions and vacuum supplies.

Our Repair Policy: One-day service whenever possible.

We offer free hands-on sewing machine classes with the purchase of a machine. Classes are held Wednesday and Saturday.
